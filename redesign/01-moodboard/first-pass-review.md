## Prompt lessons

- Strongly say: `matte wooden figures`, `no glass figures`, `no translucent figures`.
- Strongly say: `no candles`, even if asking for warmth.
- Avoid `constellation` by itself in image prompts because it triggers zodiac/star-map visuals. Prefer `wooden representatives used in family constellation workshops`.
- For room prompts, say `plain walls` and `no wall ornaments / no star maps`.
- For testimonial/note prompts, say `blank paper` rather than `handwritten notes` if generated text is not desired.
